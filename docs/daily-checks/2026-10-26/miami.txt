Miami University scholarships are highly competitive. The academic qualifications determining scholarship awards are dependent on the strength of the applicant pool.
Deadlines vary by scholarship program. All scholarships are awarded at the time of admission to Miami.
International transfer students will be considered for the transfer scholarship opportunities at the time of admission.
Scholarship awards are made at the time of admission and are not subject to change due to possible increases in tuition during the academic year. Enrolled first time undergraduate degree-seeking students will be eligible for the Miami Tuition Promise.

Please note that scholarship awards are made at the time of admission and are not subject to change due to possible increases in tuition during the academic year. Enrolled first-time undergraduate-degree seeking students will be eligible for the Miami Tuition Promise.
Application Process
All new first-year international undergraduate students with a U.S. equivalent GPA of 2.50 or higher who meet admission requirements are automatically considered for the International Merit Scholarship.
Scholarships are not guaranteed, and priority consideration is given to students who submit their application by December 1.
To be considered:
Submit a completed application, including transcript and recommendation, by the priority deadline.
Meet the minimum academic criteria.
No separate scholarship application is required. Miami does not require ACT or SAT scores for first-year applicants.
Renewal Requirements
Maintain full-time enrollment on the Oxford campus
Maintain a 3.0 cumulative GPA
Presidential Fellows Program
One of eight original Public Ivies, Miami is regarded for its unparalleled commitment to undergraduate teaching and for providing students with robust, immersive, and transformative learning experiences. In keeping with its centuries-old tradition of academic excellence, Miami is pleased to offer the Presidential Fellows Program (PFP), an elite scholarship opportunity that provides the brightest and best scholars and leaders of the 21st century with an unprecedented and exceptional college experience.
Eligibility
The Presidential Fellows Program is a highly competitive scholarship for 15 top students in the Honors College per year.
Scholarship Amount
A four-year, renewable scholarship covering tuition, fees, housing, and food.
Tuition and fees include all instructional, non-resident surcharge, basic general, career development, and course fees that you are charged.
A one-time $5,000 academic enrichment stipend.
Application Process
To be considered, apply to Miami by 11:59 p.m. on December 1, making sure to indicate your interest for Miami’s Honors College and the Presidential Fellows Program. PFP finalists will be notified in January.
Finalists will be reviewed based on their overall admission application, and an invitation-only finalist interview with the scholarship selection committee.
This scholarship is not available to spring applicants. To find out more, visit the Presidential Fellows Program page.
Renewal Requirements
Maintain full-time enrollment on the Oxford campus
Maintain a 3.5 cumulative GPA
#YouAreWelcomeHere Scholarship
The #YouAreWelcomeHere Scholarship recognizes promising international students with a vision for enhancing intercultural understanding. As a participating institution in this program, Miami will award two annual, renewable scholarships.
Eligibility
To qualify, you must hold citizenship in a country outside the United States and also not possess U.S. citizenship or permanent residency.You must also demonstrate interest and personal initiative in activities involving intercultural learning and exchange.
Application Process
Apply for admission to Miami as a first-year student. Priority consideration is given to students who apply by December 1.
Submit the #YouAreWelcomeHere application by January 1. The #YouAreWelcomeHere Scholarship application is available on the Applicant Status page after you are admitted to Miami.
Scholarship recipients will be notified in February.
If you are awarded the #YouAreWelcomeHere Scholarship, you must submit your enrollment deposit to Miami by May 1 to receive the scholarship.
Scholarship Amount
The #YouAreWelcomeHere Scholarship covers a minimum of 50 percent of the cost of tuition.
Renewal Requirements
Maintain full-time enrollment on the Oxford campus
Maintain a cumulative 3.0 GPA
Prodesse Scholarship
Miami awards the Prodesse Scholarship to select top students admitted to the Honors College who aren't also Presidential Fellows.
Application Process
To be considered, apply to Miami through the Common Application by 11:59 p.m. on December 1, making sure to indicate your interest for Miami’s Honors College.
Scholarship Amount
The Prodesse Scholarship offers $1,000 to $2,000 per year and is renewable for up to four years.
Renewal Requirements
Maintain full-time enrollment on the Oxford campus
Maintain a cumulative 3.0 GPA
