Provider: Iowa State University
Study destination: United States
Study level: Bachelor
Award
First-year USD6,000–14,000/year up to four years; transfer USD4,000–8,000/year up to three.
Eligibility
Incoming international-tuition first-year or transfer students; GPA/test criteria vary.
Eligible African countries: Algeria, Angola, Benin, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Burundi, Cabo Verde, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Comoros, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Republic of the Congo, Côte d’Ivoire, Djibouti, Egypt, Equatorial Guinea, Eritrea, Eswatini, Ethiopia, Gabon, Gambia, Ghana, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Kenya, Lesotho, Liberia, Libya, Madagascar, Malawi, Mali, Mauritania, Mauritius, Morocco, Mozambique, Namibia, Niger, Nigeria, Rwanda, São Tomé and Príncipe, Senegal, Seychelles, Sierra Leone, Somalia, South Africa, South Sudan, Sudan, Tanzania, Togo, Tunisia, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe. All other academic, residency and programme requirements also apply.
Application timing
Automatic; accept admission by May 1 for fall or December 15 for spring.
Cycle: Ongoing automatic program; Spring/Fall 2027 admissions
