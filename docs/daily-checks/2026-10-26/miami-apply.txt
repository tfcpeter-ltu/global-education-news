Admission Requirements for All International Applicants
Application Fee
Select "Miami Fee Waiver" on the undergraduate application to waive our $70 application fee when you apply by April 1 for Fall 2026.
Academic Records
Academic records include all secondary school studies, beginning with 9th grade through the most current available at the time of application.
Final and official secondary school academic transcripts along with proof of secondary school graduation is required before enrolling at Miami.
Students who completed secondary school two or more years ago must also submit any and all external examination results (IGCSE, GCE, WASSCE, KCSE, etc.) as issued by the examining board with their applications. Official or verified results may also be requested at the time of application.
Proof of English Language Proficiency
TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo test scores or an approved alternative proof of English language proficiency is required at the time of application. Additional English proficiency information.
Copy of Your Passport or National Identification Card
Financial Estimate Form
The Financial Estimate Form will be available after submitting an admission application. The form can be found and completed on the student Application Status page.
Financial Estimate Form
Required Documents for Visa
An I-20 visa document is required to obtain a student visa and to enroll as an international student at Miami University. To receive yours, please first provide the following items:
Completed online Financial Support Form.
Documentation of financial support, such as a bank statement dated not more than one year prior to your intended date of entry. (Tax returns or letters of employment/income are not acceptable.)
Additional Admission Information
For First-Year Applicants
ACT or SAT Scores (optional)
ACT or SAT scores are optional, but you may use them to satisfy English proficiency requirements.
Recommendation (optional)
Your recommendation may be a letter, report from a school official, or teacher evaluation.
College of Creative Arts Requirements
Some majors in the College of Creative Arts have portfolio, audition, or video requirements for admission.
Honors College
You may identify your interest in admission to Miami's Honors College on your admission application.
For Transfer Applicants
Midterm report and/or recommendations
If you’re in your first university term, we recommend submitting a mid-term report [PDF] with current grades and/or a recommendation from a university instructor. You may email these optional materials to GoGlobal@MiamiOH.edu.
College of Creative Arts and Farmer School of Business Transfer Requirements
Some majors in the College of Creative Arts have portfolio, audition, or video requirements for admission.
The Farmer School of Business has additional, minimum requirements for transfer admission.
Honors College
You may identify your interest in admission to Miami's Honors College on your admission application.
